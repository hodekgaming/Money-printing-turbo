Background music tracks (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.flac`).

"Random" in the UI picks one of the files in this folder. The two `demo_*.mp3`
pads are synthesized by `scripts/make_demo_music.py` and are free to use.
For better results add royalty-free music you have the rights to, e.g. from the
YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay Music. Tracks loop automatically if shorter
than the video, and fade out at the end.
